# Sign-in and alerting notes

## Face unlock after a credential change

Face unlock stores a device-bound token that is issued the first time a customer
enrols. Changing the account password invalidates that token, because the token
is signed with a key derived from the password hash. The application does not
tell the customer this. It shows the same generic "we could not verify you"
screen it shows for a failed scan, so a customer whose enrolment has been
invalidated will keep retrying the scan instead of re-enrolling. The fix is to
sign in once with the new password, which issues a fresh token; after that,
face unlock works again on that device only.

## Why a one-time code sometimes arrives late

One-time codes are queued through two carriers. The second carrier is used when
the first reports congestion, and its delivery target is ninety seconds rather
than five. A code that arrives after the sign-in screen has timed out is still
valid for three minutes, so entering it on a fresh screen will succeed. Support
sees this most often between 09:20 and 09:40 on weekdays, which is when the
first carrier is busiest.

## Price alert timing

Price alerts are evaluated on the quote stream, not on the order book. The quote
stream is aggregated into one-second buckets, and the alert service reads the
completed bucket. An alert therefore fires at least one second after the price
was first printed, and up to three minutes later when the alert service is
rebalancing across nodes. Customers who trade on alerts should treat them as
notification of a move that has already happened, not as a trigger.

## Watchlists and releases

Watchlists live in the account profile, not on the device. A release that
migrates the profile schema rebuilds every watchlist from the previous
representation; if a customer opened the application during the migration window,
their client may have written an empty watchlist back over the rebuilt one.
Recovering it means restoring the profile snapshot taken before the release,
which support can do for thirty days afterwards.
