Incident summary: checkout failures

Impact lasted 41 minutes. The billing host stopped accepting connections on
port 5432 while the hostname continued to resolve normally, so the first
responder spent 20 minutes checking DNS before testing reachability.

Root cause: the database container was evicted during a node drain and the
scheduler had no capacity left in that availability zone.

Follow-up: add a reachability probe ahead of the DNS probe in the runbook.
